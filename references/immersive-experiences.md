# Immersive and dimensional websites

Read for new presentation-led work under this skill, especially image-rich marketing sites, portfolios, dimensional panels and scroll choreography. Use the quality and asset-production approach by default for open briefs; adjust expression to the business and respect an explicitly restrained direction.

## Define the journey before effects

Map the opening, exploration, key transitions and final action. Specify how the visitor experiences each scene while scrolling normally. Keep substantive imagery and graphics throughout, rather than placing all ambition in a logo and leaving the rest as standard text sections.

Visual richness belongs inside the business story. Do not insert a dedicated art toy, shape laboratory, rotation slider or material selector merely to prove that the site supports 3D. Before adding a panel, state what visitors can understand or do better because it exists. If its only purpose is “play with this shape,” omit it unless a genuine product viewer/configurator or the explicit brief requires that behavior. Keep visual ambition in the imagery, depth, transitions and useful panels instead.

Choose a coherent visual world: materials, lighting, depth, movement and a connecting motif. A continuous ribbon or line can weave between scenes and change orientation with scroll progress. Implement it as a genuinely connected path or object; do not fake continuity with unrelated decorative strokes at each section boundary. Keep it away from important text and controls.

## Supply the visual substance

Plan and create suitable raster artwork as part of the build whenever it improves the composition; do not wait for a second request for images. Integrate it prominently and inspect crops at real breakpoints. Do not substitute generic boxes, a few gradients, or the existing logo for the image work. Keep generated illustrations distinct from evidence of real client work. Preserve source artwork and export efficient derivatives.

Combine illustration with functioning browser-native or live-3D layers where useful. Use physical cues coherently: thickness, occlusion, lighting, reflections, shadows, perspective and relative movement. A flat rectangle with a drop shadow does not automatically provide convincing dimensionality.

## Make panels genuinely interactive

Use interactions where they advance exploration: inspect a real project, switch a featured composition, expand a capability, compare relevant options or explore the actual build stages. Each surface should have a clear action and meaningful response. Do not impose a panel count or WebGL requirement; retain distinctive original assets and strong composition even when the journey needs few controls. Avoid repeating the same hover tilt on every card and calling that interaction depth. Include object manipulation only when seeing that actual object from different angles serves the visitor's task.

Keep keyboard and touch paths. Hover embellishments complement explicit buttons and accessible controls. Preserve real destinations, factual content and the primary enquiry/booking journey. Do not invent product features, project outcomes or unrelated app functions to populate panels.

## Engineer the choreography

Prefer a coordinated renderer or a small number of deferred scenes over a new WebGL context per card. Update scroll-linked transforms in a bounded animation loop, rebuild geometry on layout changes rather than every frame, cap pixel ratio and pause offscreen or hidden animation. Preserve native scrolling; sticky scenes must not trap the visitor.

Match scroll-linked rotation, translation and progress to actual section positions. Recalculate after image loads, filtering, disclosure changes and viewport resizing. A continuous line should remain connected at those boundaries. Reserve space for media and avoid animation that destabilizes layout.

Provide a considered compact-device version with imagery and interaction still present. Use an efficient 2D/2.5D rendering when needed, rather than silently dropping every graphic on mobile. Reduced motion should retain a strong static composition and all essential information and controls.

## Prove the result visually and behaviorally

Inspect the initial frame, intermediate scroll positions, section transitions and the final scene on desktop and phone. Observe that the connecting motif moves and rotates as intended, and that each interactive panel actually changes state. Check that generated images load sharply, content stays readable, controls remain reachable and no canvas or perspective layer creates overflow. Verify pause/reduced motion and recheck existing navigation and contact flows.

Performance measurements support this review; they do not replace the art direction or interaction checks. Improve expensive effects without reducing the requested experience to a basic template.
